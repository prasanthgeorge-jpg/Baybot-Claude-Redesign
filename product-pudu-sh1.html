Key Features

What Sets the PUDU SH1 Apart

Smart, hands‑free floor care

This autonomous 32‑inch floor scrubbing robot delivers deep cleaning for large spaces with a single pass.

Lidar and 3D sensors map your environment, helping the bot safely avoid people, walls, and obstacles.

Multiple cleaning modes let you handle daily maintenance or heavy‑duty scrubbing without manual effort.

Real‑time status monitoring and automatic docking keep your floors spotless with minimal supervision.

How It Works

Core Functions

Function 1

Hybrid Scrubbing and Drying Performance

Combines the powerful cleaning action of a walk-behind scrubber dryer with automated operation to deliver deep scrubbing and fast drying in a single pass, ensuring spotless and safe floors across large commercial spaces

Function 2

Enhanced Productivity with Autonomous Operation

Automates traditional walk-behind cleaning processes to reduce manual effort while increasing coverage speed and consistency, enabling facilities to maintain high cleanliness standards with fewer resources

Function 3

Smart AI-Powered Operational Support

Utilizes intelligent automation to assist construction teams with monitoring, guidance, and workflow optimization. By reducing human error and improving response time, it ensures safer operations, higher productivity, and smarter project execution .
