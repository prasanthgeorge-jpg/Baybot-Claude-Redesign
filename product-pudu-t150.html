About the PUDU T150

The PUDU T150 is a compact autonomous industrial delivery robot designed for efficient light-payload transportation in manufacturing and warehouse environments.

Deployed In

  • Automotive
  • Warehousing
  • Electronics

Key Features

What Sets the PUDU T150 Apart

Industrial-Grade Certified

Meeting rigorous ISO 3691-4 standards and holding industrial-specific CE certification, this robot ensures operational safety and production stability through professional-grade compliance and quality assurance.

Easily Adapts to Complex Environments

Integrating dual RGBD cameras and 360° dual LiDAR with AI-driven multi-level navigation and obstacle avoidance, this robot ensures precise positioning and seamless, stable operation in complex industrial environments.

How It Works

Core Functions

Function 1

24/7 Continuous Operation

With rapid battery swapping, auto-charging, and wired power options, the T150 delivers 24/7 uptime. This flexible power ecosystem optimizes factory throughput and maximizes production ROI.

Specifications

Technical Details

Intuitive Operation, Instant Usability

The T150 features an onboard touchscreen powered by a proprietary OS for direct task dispatching, mode switching, and configuration, supporting button pagers, PUDU Link, and API integration for intuitive, efficient operation.

Aerospace-Grade Unibody Design

High-strength aerospace materials and unibody design ensure superior impact resistance. Built for intensive duty cycles, it guarantees 5 years of reliable, high-efficiency operation.
