Ordering

How to Order a Part

Give us the model and serial number from the plate on your unit and describe the part. We'll confirm compatibility, price and lead time before anything is shipped — which avoids the most common failure mode in robot servicing, namely the right part for the wrong revision.

  • Compatibility checked against your serial number
  • Price and lead time confirmed before dispatch
  • Fitting guidance from the service team where needed
