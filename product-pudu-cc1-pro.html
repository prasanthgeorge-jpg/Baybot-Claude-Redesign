PUDU CC1 Pro

Autonomous Multi-Function Floor Cleaning Robot for Commercial Facilities

The PUDU CC1 Pro is a professional-grade autonomous cleaning robot designed for demanding commercial environments. It integrates sweeping, scrubbing, vacuuming, and mopping into a single intelligent system, delivering consistent, high-quality floor cleaning.

About the PUDU CC1 Pro

AI-powered 4-in-1 commercial cleaning robot (sweeps, scrubs, vacuums, mops) with real-time rear camera stain detection and automatic spot re-cleaning. 1500-3000㎡/h coverage with up to 9h runtime (silent mopping); 15L clean/15L waste tanks with optional auto-refill docking station
