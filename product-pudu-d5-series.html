Key Features

What Sets the PUDU D5 Series Apart

Intuitive Interaction & Smart Follow

Multimodal Interaction

D5 combines voice and gesture control with 10+ natural gestures, using deep-vision sensing and an 6-mic array. AI noise reduction and sound localization ensure accurate responses even in noisy environments.

Intelligent Follow

With dynamic obstacle avoidance and trajectory prediction, D5 follows users precisely in crowded spaces, adjusting its path in real time for smooth and safe movement.

How It Works

Core Functions

Function 1

Remote Vision & Control, Always in Command

RCOS Remote Control System: Monitor real-time operating status and issue commands instantly. Video Feed: Live panoramic views from cameras give you full visibility of the on-site environment.

Function 2

All-Weather Durability and Extreme Resilience

D5 series supports cold-start at -10°C, operates from -20°C to 55°C, and achieves IP67 ingress protection for dependable performance in rain, snow, dust, and fluctuating climate conditions.

Specifications

Technical Details

PUDU D5

Standing Dimensions 900 × 543 × 572 mm (35.4* 21.4*22.5 in) Weight (with Battery) 61 kg (134.5 lbs) Computing Platform NVIDIA Jetson Orin AGX 64GB + RK3588 Perception Sensors Dual 3D LiDAR + Four Fisheye Cameras Maximum Joint Torque 165 Nm (121.7 lb·ft) Battery Capacity 1000 Wh Operating Time 2 ~ 2.5 h Continuous Walking Payload 20–30 kg (44–66 lbs) Climbing Capability Up to 80 cm (Up to 31.5 in) Maximum Step Height 30 cm (11.8 in) Slope Capability 25° ascent, 45° descent Operating Temperature –20°C to 55°C (–4°F to 131°F) Cold-Start Capability Below –10°C (Below 14°F) Ingress Protection IP67 Autonomous Navigation Supported Autonomous Following Supported Voice Interaction Suppor
