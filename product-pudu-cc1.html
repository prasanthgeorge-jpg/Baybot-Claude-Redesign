Key Features

What Sets the PUDU CC1 Apart

Wet scrubbing and dry sweeping

Seamlessly performs wet scrubbing and dry sweeping within a single system, ensuring thorough removal of dust, spills, and stains across diverse floor surfaces.

AI-driven sensors automatically recognize different floor materials and adjust cleaning modes in real time to deliver safe, efficient, and optimized results.
Uses intelligent mapping and localization to navigate complex environments, ensuring complete coverage while smoothly transitioning between multiple floor types.
How It Works

Core Functions

Function 1

Smart Dual-Mode Cleaning

Automatically performs dry sweeping and wet mopping in a single cleaning cycle. The robot intelligently controls brush rotation, suction, and water flow to remove dust, debris, spills, and stains efficiently without manual intervention.

Function 2

Intelligent Surface Adaptation

Detects different floor types and surface conditions in real time and dynamically adjusts cleaning pressure, speed, and moisture level. This ensures effective cleaning while protecting delicate surfaces and preventing over-wetting.
