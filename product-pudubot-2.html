About the PuduBot 2

Universal delivery robot with marker-less PUDU VSLAM+ navigation for high-ceiling (30m) and extra-large spaces (40,000㎡) with 75% faster deployment. Four delivery modes (Delivery, Birthday, Cruise, Dish Return) with 10-24h LFP battery life, 3h auto-charging, and 80cm path clearance
