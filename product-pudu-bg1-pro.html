How It Works

Core Functions

Function 1

Autonomous Sweeping & Scrubbing

  • Performs dry debris removal and wet floor scrubbing simultaneously in a single pass
Function 2

Intelligent Route Planning

  • Automatically maps the environment and optimizes cleaning paths for maximum coverage
Function 3

Adaptive Cleaning Control

  • Detects dirt levels and adjusts water flow, pressure, and cleaning intensity in real time
Function 4

Edge & Corner Cleaning

  • Actively cleans along walls, edges, and tight spaces to ensure full-area coverage
Specifications

Technical Details

Performance

Cleaning Mode: Sweeping + Scrubbing (2-in-1)

Cleaning Efficiency: Up to ~10,000–15,000 sq.ft/hour (varies by environment)

Max Runtime: Up to 6–8 hours continuous operation

Cleaning Width: ~20–28 inches (approx.)

Water System

  • Clean Water Tank Capacity: ~70–80 liters
  • Waste Water Tank Capacity: ~60–70 liters
  • Automatic Water Management: Yes (with docking station)
