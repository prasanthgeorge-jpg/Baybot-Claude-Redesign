About the PUDU MT1 Max

AI-powered 3D robotic sweeper with 3D LiDAR + multi-sensor fusion for accurate mapping and real-time obstacle avoidance in complex environmentsHandles all debris types (cigarette butts to large bottles) with 70cm sweeping width, 35L trash capacity, and edge-to-edge (0cm) cleaningAdaptive cleaning adjusts power/posture automatically; covers 2200㎡/h standard, 7000㎡/h spot cleaning with 5-10h runtime (8h standard)
